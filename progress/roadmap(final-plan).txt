# AI Backend Engineering Roadmap

## Objective

Become capable of designing, implementing, securing, deploying and maintaining production backend systems.

The primary objective is not to learn frameworks. The objective is to become a backend engineer capable of building systems from scratch.

Every topic should contribute directly toward this goal.

Quick Deploy Agents is the flagship learning and portfolio project. The
architecture document is a north star, not a mandate to build every distributed
system component before the preceding phase is understood.

## Checkpoint and completion standard

Each phase deliverable is a checkpoint. Complete a checkpoint only when you
can predict its behavior, build or modify a small version without a tutorial,
verify a success and failure case, explain one alternative with its tradeoff,
and pass a short oral quiz.

Testing, validation, secret handling, and threat awareness are continuous
practices. Their dedicated later phases deepen them; they are not permission to
ignore them earlier.

# Learning Method

Every feature follows the same cycle.

1. Identify the capability required.
2. Predict the behavior and failure modes.
3. Implement one production-quality solution yourself.
4. Verify success and failure cases.
5. Explain why it works.
6. Study competing approaches and their tradeoffs.
7. Pass a short quiz, then continue building.

Example

Need authentication

↓

Implement JWT

↓

Study sessions

↓

Study cookies

↓

Study OAuth

↓

Continue

Not every technology is implemented immediately, but every major concept is understood.
